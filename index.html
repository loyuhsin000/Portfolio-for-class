Alpine Observatory & Refuge

Anchored within high-altitude granite bedrock, the sanctuary integrates locally extracted stone aggregates into stratified concrete walls. It withstands severe glacial winds while framing an uninterrupted gaze upon the mountain landscape.

Back to Earth

Subterranean Geothermal Core

Two-thirds of the volumetric footprint is carved directly into the volcanic coastal topography. The surrounding soil mass provides natural passive insulation, while filtered skylights illuminate the raw board-formed concrete chambers.

Spatial Practice & Bio

Kaelen Vance is an architectural researcher and designer focusing on cold-climate vernacular tectonics, mass timber structures, and site-excavated monolithic concrete.

Our practice approaches architecture not as decorative enclosure, but as an enduring geological dialogue between topographical gravity and atmospheric light.
